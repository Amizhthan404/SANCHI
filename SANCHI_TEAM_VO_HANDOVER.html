🎙️ Crucial Voice-Over Guidelines for All 6 Teammates

Please read these before tapping record on your mobile phone or computer mic:

1. Mobile Phone Mic Positioning Hold your phone 5 to 6 inches away from your mouth at a 45° angle (near your cheek). Do not speak straight into the mic to prevent breath pops ("p", "t", "b").
2. Silence Room Noise Turn off ceiling fans, AC, coolers, and close room windows. Avoid rooms with bare walls (record facing a wardrobe, curtain, or bed to absorb echo).
3. Recommended Recording Apps iPhone: Voice Memos (Settings → Lossless audio).
Android: Dolby On (Free) or default Voice Recorder set to High Quality WAV.
4. Pacing & Energy Speak at ~130 to 140 words per minute (about 80% your natural casual speed). Pronounce technical names clearly: "MPLADS", "e-SAKSHI", "PFMS", "SANCHI".
