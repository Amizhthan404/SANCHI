Statistical Anomaly Detection Methodology

1. Z-Score Analysis

National-level statistical outlier detection using Z = (x−μ)/σ. MPs with |Z| > 2 are flagged as allocation anomalies.

2. IQR Fencing

Interquartile Range method. Values beyond Q3 + 1.5×IQR or below Q1 − 1.5×IQR are flagged as outliers.

3. Peer Benchmark

State-level peer group comparison. MPs deviating more than 35% from their state mean are reviewed.

4. Milestone Divergence

Compares PFMS fund releases vs recorded physical milestone completion to catch rapid disbursement before work.

5. Asset Verification

Tracks field inspection records for high-value completed projects, flagging unverified assets for audit.

Multi-Source Ingestion Pipelines & Communication Gateways

Sanchi synthesizes live streams across four core government architectures to eliminate institutional data silos and monitor full lifecycle fund utilization:

PFMS (Public Financial Management System) CONNECTED
Stream: Ministry releases, State nodal distributions, DBT vendor payments, bank transaction vouchers.
Surveillance Focus: Expenditure velocity anomalies, unspent balances, payment gaps.
e-SAKSHI Portal (MoSPI) SYNCED
Stream: Hon'ble MP work recommendations, District Collector administrative sanctions, technical clearances.
Surveillance Focus: Duplicate work orders, sanction delays, guideline compliance.
GeM & State e-Tenders INDEXED
Stream: Implementing agency work orders, contractor bids, completion certificates.
Surveillance Focus: Contractor repeat-win rate, bidding collusion, cost overruns (>110%).
NIC SMS & Alert Gateway ONLINE • 99.9% SLA
Stream: Automated escalation notices to District Collectors, State Nodal Officers, and Hon'ble MP secretariats.
Delivery Protocols: Government NIC SMS push, encrypted webhooks, and audit logs.

Statutory Compliance & Legal Policies

1. Official Terms of Portal Access & Governance

This surveillance portal is operated under the auspices of the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India, for automated compliance monitoring and fraud prevention under the Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS).

2. Authorised User Obligations & Legal Penalties

Access is restricted strictly to designated personnel including Ministry Administrators, State Nodal Officers, District Collectors/Magistrates, and Members of Parliament. Unauthorised access, tampering, or misuse of scheme surveillance records constitutes an offence punishable under Sections 43, 66, and 72 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, in addition to relevant sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).

3. Auditability & Session Tracking

All activities on this portal—including query filters, case dossier triage actions, CSV export events, and SMS escalation notices—are timestamped, cryptographically hashed, and recorded in immutable government audit journals.

1. Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

The Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation is committed to protecting the privacy of public representatives, implementing agencies, and citizens. Data processed within Sanchi adheres strictly to the principles of purpose limitation, data minimisation, and storage limitation under the DPDP Act, 2023.

2. Collection of Scheme Records & Role Scoping

Information collected comprises official public records relating to MPLADS fund recommendations, administrative sanctions, and PFMS payment disbursements. Jurisdictional filters guarantee that officers only view and triage records within their statutory remit (National, State, or District level).

3. Redaction and Log Retention

Personally Identifiable Information (PII) of beneficiaries or non-gazetted staff is masked or pseudonymised prior to presentation. Audit logs are retained for statutory periods in compliance with the Public Records Act, 1993.

1. Links to External Government Portals

Sanchi provides direct diagnostic and cross-reference links to official Government of India repositories including the Public Financial Management System (PFMS - pfms.nic.in), e-SAKSHI Portal (esakshi.mospi.gov.in), Government e-Marketplace (GeM), and Sansad Portal (sansad.nic.in).

2. External Link Disclaimers

MoSPI is not responsible for the contents or reliability of linked external websites and does not necessarily endorse the views expressed within them. External portals govern their own availability and terms of usage.

1. STQC Certification Standards

This software architecture is designed in strict conformance with the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0) and Standardisation Testing and Quality Certification (STQC) security baselines.

2. Encryption & Transport Layer Security

All communication channels are protected with TLS 1.3 encryption employing high-grade AES-256 ciphers. API communications between the front-end dashboard and the analytical database are protected with strict Content Security Policy (CSP) headers and cross-site scripting (XSS) mitigations.

3. CERT-In Incident Reporting

Any anomalous access pattern or suspected intrusion attempt is automatically flagged and reported to the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under statutory notification mandates.
